Section 04

English Department Test Writing Standards

Mandatory formatting and pedagogical rules to ensure clarity, fairness, and rigor across all quizzes.

Rule 1.1

Section Headings & Continuous Numbering

  • Include a clear heading with the section letter, question type, and marks.
  • Write a short, simple instruction directly under every heading.
  • Number all questions continuously from 1 to 18 (or 1 to 20 for 11AS/12A2)—do not restart numbering at 1 in new sections.
Compliant Heading Example

Section A – Literature (Multiple Choice) [2 Marks]

Choose the correct answer for each of the following questions.

Rule 1.2

Key Words: Bold & Underlined

In every question, the critical focus word(s) or negative qualifiers that guide student comprehension must be both bold and underlined.

Approved Question Prompts
1. Which sentence is NOT a fragment?
2. Find the adverb clause in the sentence below.
Rule 1.3

Vocabulary & Grammar: Test Usage, Not Definitions

  • Vary question types across vocabulary and grammar (avoid repeating the exact same item format).
  • Focus on application in context—students must apply the word or grammatical rule inside a meaningful sentence.
AVOID (Rote Recall) "What is the definition of an adjective clause?"
DO THIS (Contextual) "Choose the sentence that correctly uses an adjective clause."
Rule 1.4

General Quality & Accuracy Checks

Grade-Appropriate Language

Ensure sentence complexity, passage length, and vocabulary match the cognitive level of your grade.

Unambiguous Answers & Complete Key

Verify that there is strictly one correct answer per item and prepare a full Answer Key alongside every quiz.

Section 05 • Rule 1.5

Submission Workflow

Follow these four steps in sequence before administering the quiz in Weeks 16–17.

1

Draft Quiz & Answer Key

Write the quiz and complete answer key adhering strictly to the covered Weeks 12–13 material and formatting rules.

2

Upload to Google Drive

Upload both the Quiz file and the Answer Key to the English Department shared Drive folder for HOD review.

3

Apply Review Corrections

Promptly implement any edits or feedback requested after the departmental review.

4

Publish Revision on Schoology

Upload the quiz revision material to Schoology before the break starts so students can prepare.

Reminder: Schoology revision materials must go live prior to the break!
